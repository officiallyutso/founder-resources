# Blog seeds, 18 November 2026

Drawn from pages opened for the Daily Founder Brief of that date. No prospect names, no comments, no pipeline.

1. **Working title:** A world average standing in for a heat
   **Register:** research
   **Thesis:** The 16 November CER comparison of downstream CBAM lists sorts finished goods with 1.9 tonnes of CO2 per tonne of steel, and the author states that this is a global average, not a plant bill.
   **Source:** Elise Viadere, Centre for European Reform, 16 November 2026
   **URL:** https://www.cer.eu/insights/where-extending-eus-carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism-make-sense
   **Why it is worth a post:** It is a concrete picture of a carbon number that travels without the furnace that made it, which is the document an exporting plant will eventually be asked to replace.
   **Must not be claimed:** That 30 November is confirmed on an EU calendar, that any product list is in force, that 1.9 tonnes per tonne describes an Indian forge, or any Stamped role in the calculation. The WTO panel and the free-permit date are her reporting.

2. **Working title:** The stage changed. The dates on the wall did not.
   **Register:** Stamped field note
   **Thesis:** CAQM revoked GRAP Stage III on 17 November after recording Delhi's AQI at 204, while the 30 October order still runs office attendance at up to 50 percent through 20 November, and the furnace-camera date of 30 November was not part of the revocation.
   **Source:** Press Information Bureau, 17 November 2026, 7:46 pm
   **URL:** https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleseDetailm.aspx?PRID=2337619&lang=1&reg=3
   **Why it is worth a post:** It separates a headline about air quality improving from the two dates a plant manager in NCR still has on a whiteboard.
   **Must not be claimed:** That diesel-generator rules or vehicle bans were rewritten by the PIB note. Those details are in the Indian Express account of officials. That the 50 percent rule applies to a furnace crew. The release says offices. District AQI figures are the Express's 9 pm readings, not the committee's 204.

3. **Working title:** A new die-casting line and a 65 percent sentence
   **Register:** business essay
   **Thesis:** Uno Minda's November 2026 deck reports aluminium die casting up 65 percent with capacity added in Bawal and Hosur, and says the HPDC alloy-wheel plant at Bawal has commenced, in a quarter where consolidated EBITDA margin is 11.0 percent against 11.5 percent.
   **Source:** Uno Minda investor presentation, titled November 2026
   **URL:** https://www.unominda.com/uploads/investor/Invites_announcement/Uno%20Minda%20Investor%20Presentation%20Q2%20FY27.pdf
   **Why it is worth a post:** The first months of a high-pressure line are when a new die and a new thermal window write the scrap, and the deck publishes the growth without that scrap.
   **Must not be claimed:** A rejection rate, a cause for the 44 basis point margin gap, or that "commenced" means every planned line is running. The project table marks the Bawal 2W alloy-wheel expansion as partly commissioned.

4. **Working title:** The open window was a later draft
   **Register:** research
   **Thesis:** Dattani's column argues that the popular accident stories for penicillin, Pasteur's vaccine and X-rays sit on thin or contradictory records, and that at least one of them was kept simple in public because the method was easy to copy.
   **Source:** Saloni Dattani, Works in Progress, 16 November 2026
   **URL:** https://worksinprogress.co/issue/accidental-discoveries/
   **Why it is worth a post:** It is a clean way to talk about why a plant improvement needs a notebook: the dinner-party version arrives after the settings are gone.
   **Must not be claimed:** That she settled the Fleming chronology. She agrees with Hare after reading his archive, and she says so. The byline is on the magazine front. The essay page itself did not print it in the text opened here.

5. **Working title:** Three sigma is a reason to look
   **Register:** research
   **Thesis:** Wood reports that the Dark Energy Survey's final analysis prefers a changing dark energy at three sigma, and he states that this is short of the five sigma required to call a discovery.
   **Source:** Charlie Wood, Quanta Magazine, 16 November 2026
   **URL:** https://www.quantamagazine.org/what-makes-up-the-cosmos-a-20-year-survey-hints-that-something-is-missing-20261116/
   **Why it is worth a post:** It gives a non-slogan scale for how hard a plant should treat a drift flag: interesting enough to open the chart, not enough to rewrite the standard.
   **Must not be claimed:** That cosmologists have abandoned Lambda-CDM, or that the 17 November correction changed the three-sigma sentence. Wood also reports an alternative that moves an early-universe date. That alternative stays in any retelling.

6. **Working title:** People play the score that is on the wall
   **Register:** research
   **Thesis:** Kim argues that games and character-types work because they tell a person the next move and what counts as doing it well, and that this efficiency can capture the definition of success.
   **Source:** Hannah Kim, Asterisk via Substack, 12 November 2026
   **URL:** https://asteriskmag.substack.com/p/we-are-all-playable-characters
   **Why it is worth a post:** A shift will chase the number it can see. Tonnes out the door and good parts per heat are different games.
   **Must not be claimed:** That she studied factories, or that Nguyen's "value capture" line is an empirical result. It is her use of his philosophy. The Substack date is 12 November 2026. The print issue is Asterisk 16.
