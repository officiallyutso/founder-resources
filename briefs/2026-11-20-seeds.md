**Seed 1. The gas month and the heat number.** Register: Stamped field note. Thesis: a foundry can price a crore of trials and rework and still correct the gas spike only after the quarter closes, so a single quarterly conversion cost hides the month. Source: Steelcast Q2 FY27 transcript, call 13 November 2026, filed 19 November 2026. https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/STEELCAS_19112026175749_Steelcast-Transcript_Q2FY27Merged.pdf Why it is worth a post: it is a plant diary of cost timing, readable without a carbon lecture. **Seed 4. Public model, private conversation.** Register: business essay. Thesis: a score on public trial designs and a conversation with someone who has seen the trial are different instruments.
