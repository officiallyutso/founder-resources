## BLOG SEEDS FOR THE WEEKLY EDITOR

**1. Three sizes of a record quarter**
- Register: business essay
- Thesis: One foundry quarter can be 50% in rupees, 24% after alloy, and 19% in tonnes, and the furnaces did the third.
- Source: Alicon Castalloy Q2 and H1 FY27 earnings call transcript
- Original date: 16 November 2026, filed 20 November 2026
- URL: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ALICON_20112026112608_Letter_to_SEs_for_Transcript.pdf
- Why it is worth a post: It is a public, numeric example of price, mix and pieces in a single document, which is a distinction a plant essay can teach on its own.
- Must not be claimed: that the 50% is false, that tonnes are the only number that matters for cash, that Binola produced the result alone, or any client result.

**2. Ending the piece-by-piece check**
- Register: business essay
- Thesis: A chassis ramp grew while people checked every piece, and management now wants that cost out.
- Source: Talbros Automotive Components Q2 and H1 FY27 earnings call transcript
- Original date: 12 November 2026, filed 18 November 2026
- URL: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/TALBROAUTO_18112026112349_Transcriptmergedsigned.pdf
- Why it is worth a post: 100% inspection shows up here as a temporary ramp cost with a margin attached, which is a real shop-floor trade.
- Must not be claimed: that the forging division was the line under 100% check, that the phase is safely over, or any client result.

**3. Two clocks for the first carbon auction**
- Register: research
- Thesis: A CII verifier dates the first CCTS trades to March-April 2027, while the procedure still points at the last Thursday of each month.
- Source: Ruchira Singh, S&P Global, interview with P.V. Kiran Ananth
- Original date: 12 November 2026 (interview 11 November 2026)
- URL: https://www.spglobal.com/energy/en/news-research/latest-news/energy-transition/111226-interview-indian-industries-show-uneven-carbon-market-readiness-first-trading-by-march-april-2027
- Why it is worth a post: 26 November is a public test of which clock runs, and the interview says what verifiers actually open.
- Must not be claimed: that $5 to $10 a tonne is a traded price, that a 26 November auction is impossible, or that steel targets are notified.

**4. The bottleneck moves**
- Register: tech essay
- Thesis: Throughput stays capped by the constraint, so speeding every other task leaves output where it was.
- Source: David Peterson, Angular Ventures, "The search for the AI-native firm"
- Original date: 17 November 2026
- URL: https://newsletter.angularventures.com/p/the-search-for-the-ai-native-firm
- Why it is worth a post: Goldratt's constraint, set next to a current argument about selling work rather than software, is a clean lens on any plant tool.
- Must not be claimed: the newsletter's asides on an Anthropic IPO or on Nvidia's leadership, which were not separately checked.

**5. Design the algorithm, then still pour the metal**
- Register: tech essay
- Thesis: Treating a hardware design as an algorithm can shrink the engineering loop, and the loop still ends in a physical test.
- Source: Tomas Pueyo, "Hardware Is Software"
- Original date: 18 November 2026
- URL: https://unchartedterritories.tomaspueyo.com/p/hardware-is-software
- Why it is worth a post: Scholl's line is a sharp way to think about process recipes, and Pueyo's own slower school is the brake.
- Must not be claimed: a 65% to 75% cost reduction, a robot timeline, or a change in Nvidia's leadership.

**6. Discipline that knows who it is for**
- Register: psychology
- Thesis: In industrial firms, operational discipline pays when people can name the customer it serves.
- Source: PwC Katzenbach Center, "When company culture and business strategy lock arms"
- Original date: 20 November 2026
- URL: https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/human-resources/katzenbach-culture-strategy-alignment-study.html
- Why it is worth a post: A current free study, with a specific section on industrial products, safety, and where experiment is allowed.
- Must not be claimed: that the 2.5 times figure is causal, or that a forge's culture can be scored the way the study scores Fortune 500 reviews.
