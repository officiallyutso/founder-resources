## BLOG SEEDS FOR THE WEEKLY EDITOR

**S1. The letter asks for 91 percent. The melt cares about iron.**
- Register: business essay
- Thesis: A purity floor on imported aluminium scrap is a lobby's screen, and it is a weak description of the charge a die caster actually pours.
- Source: Business Standard, "Aluminium body seeks import duty hike, says ₹4 trillion pipeline at risk," Saket Kumar; read with ET Manufacturing the same day and Recykal Times on 12 November.
- Original date: 24 November 2026 (Recykal: 12 November 2026)
- URL: https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/aluminium-body-seeks-import-duty-hike-says-4-trillion-pipeline-at-risk-126112400814_1.html
- Why it is worth a post: It is a live policy fight with a shop-floor translation, and the two public versions of the ask do not match.
- Must not claim: that any duty or 91 percent rule is in force; that ₹88,434 crore or 11.5 million tonnes is audited; that a client's scrap rate moved; that percent-aluminium equals a tramp-element spec.

**S2. Two models, one Delhi morning.**
- Register: research
- Thesis: On 24 November, two official-looking source splits gave industry a small share of Delhi's PM2.5 and gave each other almost no common categories, so the residual is the story.
- Source: IITM AIRWISE-Delhi dashboard, read against PTI as carried by ThePrint.
- Original date: 24 November 2026 (dashboard opened 25 November)
- URL: https://ews.tropmet.res.in/
- Why it is worth a post: It shows what happens when two source splits, built for different questions, get treated as one measurement.
- Must not claim: that Faridabad industry emits nothing because one morning slice shows 0.0 percent; that GRAP Stage II was revoked because a dashboard said "GRAP 1"; that the two percentages can be added.

**S3. A pattern has three geometries before it meets sand.**
- Register: tech essay
- Thesis: A foundry pattern shown off the printer, after a partial machine cut, and fully finished is three tools, and the mould only sees one of them.
- Source: IFFE organiser page. The three pattern stages were on public posts from the show floor the same week.
- Original date: 22 November 2026 (show runs 22 to 24 November)
- URL: https://iffeexpo.odoo.com/
- Why it is worth a post: Tool geometry is an upstream cause of casting drift, and the show made the three stages visible in one stall.
- Must not claim: a measured dimensional delta (the post does not give one); that 3D-printed patterns are more accurate than wood; any visitor count beyond the organiser's "expected" figures.

**S4. Repeat the part, or you are still building a project.**
- Register: business essay
- Thesis: Cost falls when the same work happens again under conditions you can hold still, and a one-off complaint never enters that curve.
- Source: Liv Jenks, "Manufacture vs. Construct," Union Square Ventures.
- Original date: 20 November 2026
- URL: https://www.usv.com/writing/manufacture-vs-construct
- Why it is worth a post: It gives a founder a way to talk about learning curves without a software metaphor.
- Must not claim: that any portfolio company named in the piece has factory data to show; that a forge automatically compounds learning just by running volume.

**S5. The salient number is the one that crowds out the others.**
- Register: psychology
- Thesis: Fast judgment follows the loudest cue and drops the context, and the slow version of thinking is mostly the discipline of keeping two numbers on the table.
- Source: Jacob Falkovich, "Thinking is Hard (for Humans)," LessWrong.
- Original date: 23 November 2026
- URL: https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/NyCFKxR74RgRbs7SM/thinking-is-hard-for-humans
- Why it is worth a post: It is a personal piece about attention, and it travels into how a founder reads a quarterly number.
- Must not claim: that the dating spreadsheet in the essay is a method for plant decisions, or that the piece is a study. It is an essay.

**S6. November's gas price is already two prices.**
- Register: business essay
- Thesis: November's domestic gas formula price and the capped price for old nomination fields are both "the gas price," and they describe different molecules.
- Source: PPAC homepage ($12.00 and the $9.89 HPHT ceiling) and The Signal, "Why Mumbai's CNG Keeps Rising While Crude Falls."
- Original date: 15 November 2026 for The Signal; PPAC page last updated 23 November 2026, price period 1 to 30 November.
- URL: https://thesignal.in/business/2026/11/15/mumbai-cng-eighth-hike-crude-falls-apm-gas-2026
- Why it is worth a post: Energy cost conversations in a forge start from a single number, and this month the official board is already carrying more than one.
- Must not claim: that a forge buys APM gas at $7 (that cap is The Signal's reading of a PPAC table, and it is a city-gas slice); that MGL's mix is a forge's mix; any client energy saving.

**S7. A capital that cannot see the shop, and a shop that cannot see the capital.**
- Register: business essay
- Thesis: Industrial policy fails in the gap where the centre lacks the region's real capacity and the region lacks the centre's actual priority.
- Source: Kyle Murphy and Jake Higdon, "What's Missing from the American Industrial Revival?," NEIS Center.
- Original date: 20 November 2026
- URL: https://neiscenter.substack.com/p/whats-missing-from-the-american-industrial
- Why it is worth a post: The same gap shows up when a national carbon or duty number is asked to stand in for a heat.
- Must not claim: that the $8 billion or the Midwest poll is an Indian figure; that the US story predicts CCTS; that a new institution, by itself, fixes a plant ledger.
