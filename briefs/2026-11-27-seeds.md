## BLOG SEEDS FOR THE WEEKLY EDITOR

**1. Working title:** The cheap hour and the capped hour
**Register:** business essay
**Thesis:** On one IEX delivery day the afternoon cleared near Rs 1.61/kWh and the evening sat on the Rs 10 cap, so a monthly power average is a lunchtime number.
**Source:** IEX Day-Ahead Market snapshot, delivery 27 November 2026, with CERC Petition No. 94/AT/2026.
**Original date:** 27 November 2026 (delivery day); CERC order 19 November 2026.
**URL:** https://www.iexindia.com/market-data/day-ahead-market/market-snapshot
**Why utso.stamped.work:** It is a clock a plant can check against its own shift, and it is a clean lesson in averages that hide the hour that matters.
**Must not claim:** That a Haryana DISCOM customer paid Rs 10. That firm renewable power at Rs 4.48/kWh is already the plant’s contract. That one day is the season. Any Stamped savings figure.

**2. Working title:** The billet moved. The frame did not.
**Register:** research
**Thesis:** On this screw press, underfill lined up with billet length, diameter and a few millimetres of sideways offset, while press-frame stiffness barely moved the defect.
**Source:** Härtel, Alimov, Jiang and Gardill, Production Engineering.
**Original date:** 18 November 2026.
**URL:** https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11740-026-01483-x
**Why utso.stamped.work:** It is a forging defect with a ranked cause, and the top of that ranking is the billet.
**Must not claim:** That the ranking transfers to an Indian production hammer. That radar in the paper is a product. That SHAP windows are shop tolerances. Any plant result from Stamped.

**3. Working title:** The festive number passenger cars did not join
**Register:** business essay
**Thesis:** Festive 2026 retail set a record while passenger-vehicle retail fell 7.16%, and about one in ten festive vehicles was electric.
**Source:** FADA 42-day festive retail release.
**Original date:** 23 November 2026.
**URL:** https://fada.in/images/press-release/16b03c447ab70cFADA_Press_Release_42%20Days%20Festive%20Vehicle%20Retail%20Data%202026_FINAL.pdf
**Why utso.stamped.work:** A component plant tooled for cars and a plant tooled for two-wheelers did not have the same festive, and the headline hides that.
**Must not claim:** A comparison with SIAM production. That the electric share is a CBAM or energy fact. Telangana’s missing three-wheeler, commercial-vehicle and tractor rows.

**Original date:** 19 November 2026.

**Must not claim:** That the role has been filled, that audits of this kind are already happening at a named vendor, or that Stamped is involved with Hero. Any savings figure.

**5. Working title:** A confident wrong temperature
**Register:** research
**Thesis:** A heat-conduction benchmark treats a precise wrong number as worse than an honest interval, because the next person cannot see the difference.
**Source:** Jack Clark, Import AI 482, summarising Lawrence Livermore’s SciResearch-Eval.
**Original date:** 23 November 2026.
**URL:** https://jack-clark.net/2026/11/23/import-ai-482-huawei-kernel-benchmarks-airborne-drone-detection-and-a-national-labs-ai-science-benchmark/
**Why utso.stamped.work:** Furnace decisions fail in exactly this way when a missing sensor is filled in with a point.
**Must not claim:** The benchmark results as Clark’s own experiment. That the finding has been shown on an Indian heat-treat furnace. Any product claim.

**6. Working title:** Ask which question the number answers
**Register:** psychology
**Thesis:** Forecasting models that match humans on ordinary questions are still unproven on conditional and causal ones, and a number is useful because it forces the crux into the open.
**Source:** Scott Alexander, “The AI Forecasting Agenda.”
**Original date:** 19 November 2026.
**URL:** https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/the-ai-forecasting-agenda
**Why utso.stamped.work:** It is a method for reading plant claims and model claims without treating the first number as the decision.
**Must not claim:** That conditional forecasting is solved. The specific political probabilities in the essay as facts.

**7. Working title:** Eight minutes, and the next step still landed
**Register:** tech essay
**Thesis:** Railway’s defenders were minutes behind an attacker moving through stolen code, and a complete account the next day is a different product from a stop during the incident.
**Source:** Gergely Orosz, The Pragmatic Engineer.
**Original date:** 19 November 2026.
**URL:** https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/the-pulse-what-can-we-learn-from-132
**Why utso.stamped.work:** The same split, explanation versus interruption, is the one that matters when a batch is still in the furnace.
**Must not claim:** That the attacker was proven to be a particular model, beyond Railway and Orosz’s account. Any parallel that names a Stamped customer or a scrap percentage.
